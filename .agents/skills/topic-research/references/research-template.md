# research.md template

Use these headings exactly, in this order. Every bullet that states a fact ends with `· [S#]`.

```markdown
# Research: <Title>

- Slug: <slug> · Duration: <30m|2h> · Researched: <YYYY-MM-DD> · Sources: <n>
- Focus (from probe): <one line>

## 1. Key concepts (dependency chain)
1. **<Concept A>**: <one-line definition> · [S1]
2. **<Concept B>** (needs A): <definition> · [S2]
…

## 2. Claims
### <Concept A>
- <claim> · [S1]
- <claim with an exact number or date> · [S3]
### <Concept B>
- …

## 3. Analogies for ELI5
1. **<Analogy>**: <how it maps> · where it breaks: <limit>
2. …   (2-3 candidates; say which you'd pick and why)

## 4. Visual metaphors
| Concept | What the animation could show | Step-by-step state changes | 3D needed? | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| <A> | <boxes stacking on a plate> | <empty → push 4 → push 6 → pop> | no | <flat structure reads best in isometric> |
| <B> | <airflow around a wing> | <…> | yes | <the thing is spatial: flow wraps around a body> |

3D is justified only when the topic is spatial: airflow, molecules, orbits, physical mechanisms, layered networks seen in depth.

## 5. Common misconceptions
- **Myth:** <…> · **Why it's wrong:** <…> · **Correct idea:** <…> · [S#]

## 6. Trade-offs / when (not) to use
(For non-tech topics: **Debates and perspectives**, naming each camp.)
- Use when: <…> · [S#]
- Avoid when: <…> · [S#]
- Pros: <…> · Cons: <…>
- Alternatives: <name: when to choose it> · [S#]

## 7. Timeline / story
- <YYYY>: <event> · [S#]
(Required for 2h; optional for 30m.)

## 8. Prerequisites and next topics
- Prerequisites: <title> (<why>), …
- Next: <title> (<why>), …
- Broader topic this is a kind/part of: <title or "none">

## 9. Suggested quiz items
- <concept>: <question idea> · answer: <…> · common wrong answer: <…> · [S#]
(8-15 ideas; the grill and the PRD pick from these.)

## 10. Gaps and conflicts
- <claims you could not verify, or where sources disagree and which you trust>
```
