# Style: chalk (chalkboard)

**What it is.** A dark green-black chalkboard with chalky strokes and handwriting: the feel of a lecture where a derivation is worked out step by step.

**What it isn't.** Not a photo-real board (no frame or eraser props), not a dark-mode theme: the board is always dark, in light and dark mode.

**Materials.** The board redefines the colour tokens (light chalk ink, pastel accents); outlines in each colour's chalk ink, a slight wobble, and a grain filter that breaks strokes up like chalk; Patrick Hand for text.

**Colour.** Fixed meanings in pastel chalk: blue, green, red, yellow keep their roles. Keep fills sparse; the board should stay mostly dark.

**Motion.** Strokes draw on; terms of a formula appear one phase at a time (`kit.seq`); numbers count.

**Do.** Show formulas being built term by term, substitutions, and the one highlighted term per step; use `text` with `mono` sparingly.
**Don't.** Fill large areas; use it for long lists or tables.

**Use for.** Formulas, derivations, proofs, worked numerical examples.
