# Style: pictogram (flat)

**What it is.** Flat, friendly infographics: solid shapes without outlines, rounded corners, generic pictogram people and icons, on a soft grey stage.

**What it isn't.** Not caricature: people are generic figures (`kit.person`) that stand for roles (a customer, an approver, a team), **never likenesses of real people**, and there are no logos or brand marks.

**Materials.** Saturated fills without outlines; pale fills (`surface`, `*-soft`) keep a thin outline so cards don't vanish; rounded rectangles; Fluent icons via `kit.icon`.

**Colour.** Fixed meanings; one accent per role or flow.

**Motion.** People and things glide between places; flows of goods, money or documents move along arrows (`along`, or an ambient `kit.loop` that can be paused); counts tick up.

**Do.** Show who does what and what moves between them: actors, handoffs, money, documents, time.
**Don't.** Put names of real people on figures as if they were portraits; draw faces with expressions that judge.

**Use for.** Business, organisations, processes, history, civics, people-centred topics.
