# Probe: Ambani vs Adani

- Duration: 30 minutes.
- Purpose: Curiosity.
- Starting point: Knows some of their companies, but not how their businesses work.
- Scope: Mukesh Ambani's Reliance vs Gautam Adani's Adani Group; businesses, growth strategies and risks. Not a personal-wealth ranking or investment recommendation.

Research focus: Explain how each business makes money, the relationship between individual leaders and their companies, group structures, cash flow, capital-intensive expansion, integration, financing and business-specific risks. Use dated, comparable evidence; distinguish Reliance consolidated figures from individual Adani listed companies, and allegations from established findings. Prioritise approachable visual comparisons over jargon, personality judgements or declaring a universal winner.
