# Ambani vs Adani: lesson PRD

- **Slug:** ambani-vs-adani
- **Folder:** Ambani-Vs-Adani
- **Duration:** 30m
- **Level:** beginner
- **Domain:** business
- **Summary:** Compare how Mukesh Ambani's Reliance and Gautam Adani's businesses earn money, expand and face risk, without confusing people, companies or cash.
- **Player version:** 1.0.0

## 1. Learner profile
- Why: Curiosity.
- Starting point: Recognises some companies but does not understand their business models.
- Solid: None demonstrated.
- Shaky: None demonstrated.
- Unknown: All seven research-chain concepts; seven intake quiz answers were not sure.
- Misconceptions detected: None asserted; do not frame uncertainty as an incorrect belief.
- Pitch: Friendly, concrete and jargon-light. Teach company boxes and customer payments first, then introduce financial labels. No investment recommendation, personal-wealth ranking or universal winner.

## 2. Learning objectives
1. Distinguish the leaders, RIL consolidated, AEL consolidated and separately reported Adani businesses.
2. Explain how fuel/chemicals, Jio, retail, ports and power earn money.
3. Separate sales, accounting earnings and operating/investing/financing cash flows.
4. Trace how integration and upfront investment can grow a business without guaranteeing immediate profit.
5. Compare risks using the right company, period, units and measure.
